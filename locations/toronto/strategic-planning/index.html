Governance & Planning · Toronto

Nonprofit strategic planning and governance in Toronto

We help leaders align strategy, governance, and philanthropy so purpose is clear, direction is shared, and organizations are ready for what’s next. For Toronto nonprofits, charities and foundations.

Book a discovery call

How we help

More than a plan: a clear path forward.

We help organizations align their strategy with the scale of their mission, turning ambitious goals into strategic plans.

Through thoughtful planning and alignment of governance, we work with leaders and boards in setting priorities, clarifying roles, and making informed decisions, with the tools and measures needed to grow while staying aligned to your mission.

Governance and planning services

Connect with us
Strategic planning+

Strategic planning is more than setting objectives. It is an opportunity to clarify purpose, strengthen relationships, and define your unique role in the community. Our co-designed planning processes result in:

  • a clear and actionable strategic plan that is approved and supported by your board
  • defined strategic priorities and objectives that can be translated into action
  • an aligned and engaged board and leadership team
  • a framework for accountability and annual review
Business planning+

Putting a strategic plan into action requires a business plan that engages staff and provides a clear roadmap. We provide guidance and facilitation through operational planning, staff engagement, board alignment, and partner communications.

Board engagement and leadership alignment+

When roles are clear and engagement is high, organizations make better decisions and create greater impact. We help leaders build effective, well-structured boards through:

  • education on governance and fiduciary roles
  • facilitation that strengthens collaboration and trust
  • practical frameworks for oversight and strategic input
Board evaluation+

Our independent evaluations provide insights on culture, board effectiveness, and governance practices. Boards gain:

  • clarity on strengths and opportunities for improvement
  • a shared understanding of roles and responsibilities
  • priorities for short- and long-term development
  • renewed alignment between board and management
Governance reviews+

In many organizations, strategy evolves faster than structure. We review board composition, committee mandates, policies, and decision-making frameworks so your governance model reflects your growth and ambition.

Strategic counsel+

For organizations navigating change or implementing ambitious strategies, we provide ongoing strategic counsel on a monthly basis.

Board recruitment+

We assess board composition, identify gaps, and recruit members whose skills, experience, and values align with your strategy, from recruitment frameworks and policies to candidate identification and onboarding.

Board retreats+

A well-planned retreat can strengthen alignment, build shared language, and renew focus. We support agenda design and facilitation so your board and leadership leave with clarity and commitment.

Co-designed services+

Every organization’s context is unique. We design services for your specific needs, whether exploring an amalgamation, navigating transitions, or undertaking organizational change.
