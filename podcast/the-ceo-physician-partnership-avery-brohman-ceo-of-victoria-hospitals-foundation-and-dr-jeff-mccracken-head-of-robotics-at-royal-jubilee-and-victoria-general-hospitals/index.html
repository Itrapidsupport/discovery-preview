The CEO-Physician Partnership: Avery Brohman, CEO Of Victoria Hospitals Foundation, & Dr. Jeff McCracken, Head Of Robotics At Royal Jubilee And Victoria General Hospitals

September 30, 2026, hosted by Douglas Nelson

A successful healthcare foundation relies on strong trust between clinical leaders and executive visionaries. Establishing an effective CEO-physician partnership can transform how hospitals engage donors and elevate patient care across an entire region. In this episode, host Douglas Nelson welcomes Avery Brohman, Chief Executive Officer at Victoria Hospitals Foundation, and Dr. Jeff McCracken, Chief Urologist and Head of Robotics at Royal Jubilee and Victoria General Hospitals. Together, they discuss how their close collaboration became the strategic catalyst behind raising tens of millions of dollars for surgical innovation. From breaking down administrative silos to uniting physicians, executives, and donors around a shared vision, Avery and Dr. McCracken share practical insights on how healthcare leaders can build lasting partnerships that redefine medical excellence.
