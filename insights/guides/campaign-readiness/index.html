Is your organization ready for a campaign?

Score your organization’s readiness across strategic clarity, leadership capacity and cultural readiness, using the questions from our Capital Campaign Diagnostic Framework.

Talk to a campaign advisor
Strategic clarity

1 / 3

Strategic clarity

  • We have a compelling case for support.
  • Our priorities for campaign funding are defined.
  • Board, leadership and advancement agree on our long-term direction.
Leadership capacity

2 / 3

Leadership capacity

  • Our board governs rather than manages.
  • Senior leaders are committed to relational fundraising.
  • We have campaign champions who model enthusiasm and credibility.
Cultural readiness

3 / 3

Cultural readiness

  • We have a culture of philanthropy, not just fundraising transactions.
  • Our community trusts our organizational leadership.
  • We are ready to change and grow, even when it is uncomfortable.
